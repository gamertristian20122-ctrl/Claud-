# Wax & Wane privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026. Applies to Wax & Wane on Android (Google Play and the itch.io APK),
Windows, Linux and Steam.

**Wax & Wane does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.**

- **No internet.** The game works offline and never connects to the internet. The Android version asks
  for one permission only: vibration.
- **No ads, no analytics, no accounts, no tracking.** There is nothing to sign up for and nothing that
  reports how you play.
- **Your saves stay on your device.** Your progress, settings and the run you saved are kept in the
  game's own folder on your phone or computer. Uninstalling the game on Android deletes them. On a PC
  they are in `%APPDATA%\WaxAndWane` (Windows) or `~/.local/share/WaxAndWane` (Linux) until you delete them.
- **Backups you control.** On Android, if you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android
  may copy the game's save file to your own Google account backup, so it comes back on a new phone.
  On Steam, if Steam Cloud is on, Steam copies the save files to your Steam account. Google and Valve
  handle those copies under their own privacy policies. The developer never receives them.
- **Sharing is up to you.** The Share button on the results screen copies a short text about your run
  (how far the candle got) to your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste it yourself.
- **Children.** The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this ever changes (for example, online leaderboards), this page will say so before the update ships,
and the game will ask you first.

Questions: write to the developer's email address shown on the game's Google Play, itch.io or Steam page.
