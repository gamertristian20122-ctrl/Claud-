TALLOW, REBORN  (concept tallow2)

Name: Tallow (same hero, same name, new body).

Pitch: A chubby little white candle with big shiny eyes and rosy cheeks, who cries wax when he's
scared and keeps walking into the dark anyway.

How health / time shows on the body:
- Height is the wax: at 100% Tallow is a tall gumdrop with a melted, drippy lip; belly rows melt away
  first (60%: shorter, one-row melted edge), then he slumps (25%: a wide stub with long wax tears
  running all the way into the dish).
- The flame shrinks with him: 7x10 -> 5x8 -> 3x5, and the wick flops over at the end.
- The brass dish fills: clean bowl at 100%, a wax puddle at 60%, wax spilling over the lip at 25%.
- His face follows the clock: bright glinting eyes at 12:00, worried eyes with teary blue glints
  around 1:00, small droopy eyes plus a sweat drop near 2:00.

What changes in the game if chosen:
- player.gd _draw: same "wax rows" idea, new shape rules. Body 13 px wide (15 at the gumdrop belly,
  was 10). Overhanging lip, wavy melted edge, a wax tear stuck on each side, two stub arms. Rows are
  removed from the belly first, so the face never melts away. The hitbox stays the dish base.
- hero/: new 19x7 dish with a bowl row and ring handle (replaces holder.png). Faces become 2x3-eye
  sets: open, blink, brave, worried, tired, hurt (> <), happy (^ ^). A smaller 7x10 flame with
  trail and flare variants. The drip sprite is reused for the side tears.
- Costumes._geo() needs a pass (the body is wider and the face sits lower). Candles.LIST still works:
  each candle keeps its 4 wax colours. Recolour the tear and cheek pixels per candle.
- Store and app icon: portrait.png at x8 = 512 px, for google_play_512 / Steam capsule art.
- Text: no renames. Journal blurb: "Tallow. Stubby, brave, a little teary. Cries wax when he's
  scared and keeps going anyway." The 25% face fits the existing Guttering low-wax state.
  An optional relic hook: "Wax Tears" (tears that drip off when hit become small wax pickups).

Self-rating of appeal at game size: 7/10. The face and gumdrop shape read instantly at 1x, and at
phone scale (x4-5) the glints, cheeks and drips make it genuinely cute.
The one weakness: without the flame and dish it is basically a marshmallow. The candle identity leans
on the flame and the dish, and at 1x the tan melted-top band reads more like a hairline than melted wax.
