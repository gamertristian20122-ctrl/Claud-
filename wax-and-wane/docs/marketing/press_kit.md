## Short description (one paragraph, for news posts)

Wax & Wane is a twin-stick roguelite where your health bar is a burning candle. You play Tallow, a small living candle in a haunted house at midnight. Your wax is your health and your clock: it burns from 12:00 to 2:00 AM, every hit melts it faster, and kills buy minutes back. Fight room to room through four floors and snuff the Hour Fiend before the clock strikes two.

## Longer description

Tallow is the last candle burning in the cellar of an old house. When the house wakes at midnight, the wax on Tallow's back is everything. The candle in the corner of the screen counts from 12:00 AM to 2:00 AM, and it's the same wax that keeps you alive. A full candle lasts about nine and a half minutes if nothing touches you. Hits melt more. Kills, cleared rooms and wax drippings buy time back. As the wax goes, Tallow gets shorter and the circle of light shrinks with it.

Each run rebuilds four floors from hand-made rooms: the Cellar, the Crypt, the Chapel and the Belfry. Relics stack into builds, three relics from the same Candle Rite unlock a set bonus, and the Smoke Altar sells power for minutes cut off your candle for the rest of the night. Five candles to play, a secret Hollow on every floor, and a nightly seeded run, Tonight's Candle, give reasons to come back.

## Features

- **The candle is the clock.** One bar is your health and your timer, and you can read it from Tallow's height and the size of your light.
- **Around 64 relics** [CHECK] that stack: Twin Wick, Sharp Wick and Ember Heart together fire two piercing flames that set everything alight.
- **Candle Rites** (set bonuses), **Keepsakes** (charged items such as the Hand Bell and the Winding Key) and the **Smoke Altar** (power for minutes).
- **32+ kinds of monster** [CHECK], marked foes that pay out wax, coins or bombs, and omens that twist each floor.
- **8 bosses** [CHECK] with versus cards, from Old Rot to the Hour Fiend.
- **Five candles**: Tallow, Tea Light, Party Candle, Altar Pillar and the hard-mode Black Taper.
- **Tonight's Candle**: one seeded house a night, the same for every player.
- **Save and continue**, keyboard and mouse, gamepad and touch [CHECK].
