# How the game was made: what to say, every time

Publishers and stores will ask. Say it first, say it the same way everywhere, and never soften it. A publisher
who would walk away over it will find out anyway (Steam prints the disclosure on the store page), and finding
out late costs the deal and your name.

## One sentence (emails, short boxes)

I made Wax & Wane on my own with Claude, an AI assistant: it wrote the code and drew the game's own pixel art
through small programs under my direction, on top of a free human-made tile set and human-made music.

## The full statement (decks, forms, store pages)

Wax & Wane has one developer, who designed it, directed it, played every build and decided what stayed.

It was built with Claude, an AI assistant. Under the developer's direction Claude wrote the game's code and
in-game text, and wrote the small programs that draw the game's own pixel art: the six candles, 22 of the 42
creatures, 13 of the 16 guardians, the relic icons, the rooms, the effects and the menus. No picture came out of
an image generator; each one is drawn by a program you can read in the game's source, and the developer looked
at every sheet and kept, changed or threw out each one.

The rest was made by people and is free to use (CC0): the dungeon tiles, 20 creatures and 3 guardians from
0x72's DungeonTileset II; the music from the Ninja Adventure pack by Pixel-boy and AAA; sound effects built
mostly from Kenney's packs. Two short jingles and a few sounds were synthesized by a program. The trailer's narrator is an AI voice.

Nothing is generated while you play. The game has no network access and collects no data.

## If they ask "is the art AI?"

Yes. Say: "Yes, the game's own art was made with an AI assistant, by programs it wrote that draw each sprite,
and I chose what stayed. The tiles and about half the creatures are a free human-made pack." Then stop. Do not
argue that it is "not really AI".

## What you must never do

- Never say or imply the art was drawn by hand.
- Never leave the question blank on Steam's content survey, itch.io's AI question or a publisher's form.
- Never pitch a publisher that has said no to AI-made content (`publishers.md` lists them).
