## The pitch in two sentences

Wax & Wane is a twin-stick roguelite where your health bar is a burning candle and the night ends at 2:00 AM.
You play Tallow, a small living candle in a haunted house: every hit melts minutes off the clock, every quick
kill pours them back, and anything powerful is paid for in time.

## The pitch in one line (email subjects, short boxes)

A roguelite where your health bar is a melting candle, and the night ends at 2:00 AM.

## A longer description

Tallow is the last candle burning in the cellar of an old house. When the house wakes at midnight, the wax on
his back is everything: it is his health and it is the clock, burning from 12:00 to 2:00 AM. Hits melt it.
Fast kills, clean rooms and drippings buy minutes back. As the wax goes, Tallow gets shorter and his circle of
light shrinks with him, so you can read how the night is going without looking at a bar.

A winning night takes about ten minutes: four floors (up to seven on a long night), three possible guardians on each,
and a secret fifth floor if you beat the Belfry by 1:00 AM. 64 relics stack into builds, and the house keeps
offering the same trade in different shapes: minutes of your candle for power. Six candles play differently,
each with its own special move. Between nights there is a Journal to fill, a diary that tells the story of the
house in 24 pages, four endings, and a seeded run every night that is the same house for everyone.

## Five key features

1. **The candle is the clock.** One bar is your health and your timer, and you can read it from the hero's
   height and the size of his light.
2. **Time is the currency.** Altars, a cracked mirror, a wax font and Tallow's own Flare Up all sell power for
   minutes. Every build is a bet on finishing sooner.
3. **A full house.** 64 relics and 6 set bonuses, 42 creatures, 16 guardians with their own entrances and
   conversations, 5 special rooms, 6 champions, 4 difficulty levels.
4. **Six candles, six ways to play.** Each has its own special move: a dash, a blink, a ground slam, a burst of
   sparks, a flare that burns your own wax, and a candle that puts itself out to hide.
5. **Ten-minute runs on a phone or a PC.** Touch, keyboard and mouse, or a gamepad. It works offline and
   collects no data.
