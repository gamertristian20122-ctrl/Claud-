## The idea

You play Tallow, a small living candle in a haunted house. His wax is his health and his clock: it burns from
12:00 to 2:00 AM. Hits melt minutes. Quick kills and clean rooms pour them back. Anything powerful is paid for in
minutes of his own candle. As the wax goes he gets shorter and his light shrinks, so the whole screen tells you
how the night is going.

## What is in it

64 relics and 6 set bonuses · 16 guardians · 42 creatures · 6 candles, each with a special move · nights of
4 to 7 floors and a secret one · 5 special rooms · a story in 24 diary pages with 4 endings · 67 achievements ·
4 difficulty levels · 3 save slots · a seeded night every day that is the same house for everyone.

## How it was made

One developer, with Claude, an AI assistant: it wrote the code and drew the game's own pixel art through small
programs under my direction, on top of a free human-made tile set (0x72's DungeonTileset II) and human-made
music (the Ninja Adventure pack). Nothing is generated while you play.
