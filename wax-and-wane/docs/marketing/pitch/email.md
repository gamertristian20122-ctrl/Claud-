Keep them this short. A scout reads the first two lines and clicks one link. Fill in the `[BRACKETS]`.
Use the publisher's own form or address from `publishers.md`. Send from an address you check every day.

## When they reply

- **"Send a build / keys."** Send the Windows zip link the same day. Ask if they want Steam keys later.
- **"What do you want from us?"** One sentence from `answers.md`. Do not add to it.
- **"What is your budget?"** "None for development. For your side I would expect translation, testing and
  porting, and I would like your view on what that costs."
- **"Is the art AI?"** The answer in `made_with.md`, word for word.
- **A contract.** Do not sign anything the same day. Read `before_you_sign.md` first, and have an adult you
  trust (a parent or guardian if you are under 18) and, if you can, a lawyer read it.
- **Silence.** Normal. Most cold pitches get none. Send the next one.
