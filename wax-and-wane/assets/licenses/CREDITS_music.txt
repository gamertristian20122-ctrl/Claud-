Why each track (measured; see tools/import_audio.py MUSIC):
  title: Darkest of the slow pieces: low register (mean pitch MIDI 61), sparse (2.0 onsets/s) and eerie (diss 0.35, no settled key), opens quietly and swells (11 dB dynamic range); 96 s, so the title never loops audibly. (Manor, Intro and Mystical were busier or brighter / more major.)
  floor_1: Cellar: the sparsest dark track (1.96 onsets/s), the most clearly minor (C minor, +0.23), the lowest register of all 45 (mean pitch MIDI 57), slow breathing swells (15 dB). Calm but heavy - the bottom of the tension ramp.
  floor_2: Crypt: a step up (2.50 onsets/s) and the most unsettled harmony of the usable tracks (diss 0.36, no clear key centre), still low (MIDI 58) and slow.
  floor_3: Chapel: sacred-sounding minor (C# minor), the highest register of the dark tracks, like a choir or organ (MIDI 75), consonant (diss 0.05), denser again (2.72 onsets/s).
  floor_4: Belfry: the most driven floor track (3.72 onsets/s, percussive share 0.25), C minor (+0.17) and dissonant (0.30); the last climb before the Hour Fiend.
  shop: Tallow Merchant: D minor but the most consonant minor track (diss 0.06), a gentle steady lilt (1.99 onsets/s), 96 s so browsing never loops; a safe room that still lives in the house.
  boss: Floor guardians (floors 1-3): D minor, low (MIDI 61), dense (3.31 onsets/s) and dissonant (0.21) - a hard switch up from the Cellar, Crypt and Chapel tracks.
  final_boss: Hour Fiend: the densest usable track in the pack (4.89 onsets/s) and one of the two most percussive (0.30) - the peak of the tension ramp.
