Why each track (measured; see tools/import_audio.py MUSIC):
  title: Haunted-manor theme; the most legato track in the pack (percussive share 0.02), slow (90 bpm), opens softly, 107 s so the title screen never audibly loops.
  floor_1: Cellar: the sparsest dark track (2.0 onsets/s), C minor, lowest register of all candidates (mean pitch MIDI 56), slow breathing swells (15.6 dB dynamic range). Calm but heavy.
  floor_2: Crypt: a step up (2.5 onsets/s) and the most unsettled harmony in the pack (highest tritone / semitone co-activation, no clear key centre), still low and slow.
  floor_3: Chapel: sacred-sounding, clearly minor (C# minor, strongest minor third of the shortlist), high register like a choir/organ (mean pitch MIDI 75), denser again (2.7 onsets/s).
  floor_4: Belfry: the most driven floor track (3.8 onsets/s, percussive share 0.19), C minor and dissonant; the last climb before the Hour Fiend.
  shop: Tallow Merchant: D minor but consonant (lowest dissonance of the minor candidates), a gentle steady lilt (2.0 onsets/s), 96 s long so browsing never loops; a safe room that still lives in the house.
  boss: Floor guardians: A minor, dense (3.4 onsets/s) and dissonant, a hard switch up from the floor tracks.
  final_boss: Hour Fiend: the densest (4.9 onsets/s) and most percussive (0.23) track in the pack - the peak of the tension ramp.
