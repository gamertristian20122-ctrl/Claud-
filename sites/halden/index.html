FAQ

Common questions

Can’t find your answer? Call or text (402) 555-0148.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

No. Our van carries a 100-gallon water tank and its own generator. We only need a parking spot with a few feet of room around the car.

How long does a detail take?

An Express Wash takes about 1.5 hours, a Full Detail about 4 hours, and a Showroom Detail most of a day. Larger vehicles and very dirty interiors can take longer.

Do I need to be home during the appointment?

Only at the start and the end. Many customers leave the keys with us and keep working. We text you when the car is ready for a walk-around.

Can you detail my car at work?

Yes, as long as your building or parking lot allows it. Since we bring everything, most office lots only need to let us park.

What happens if the weather is bad?

We don’t wash outside in rain or below 35°F. If you have a garage, we can work inside it. Otherwise we reschedule you for free.

How do I pay?

Card or cash when the job is finished and you’re happy with it. Ceramic coating appointments need a $100 deposit to hold the day.

What if I’m not satisfied?

Tell us during the walk-around and we’ll fix it on the spot. If you notice something within 48 hours, we’ll come back and make it right at no charge.

Can you remove pet hair and odors?

Yes. Pet hair removal and odor elimination are available as add-ons to any package.
