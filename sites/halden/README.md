# Halden Mobile Detailing (demo site)

A professional one-page website for a **fictional** Omaha mobile car detailer, built as a portfolio demo.

## Put it online

Drag this folder onto [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop). There's no build step.

The booking form uses Netlify Forms, so it works once the site is on Netlify. Requests appear in the Netlify dashboard under **Forms**, and visitors land on `thank-you.html`. On other hosts, or when opened from your computer, the form shows a confirmation message but doesn't send anything.

## What's on the page

- A sticky header with the phone number, a **Book now** button and a mobile menu
- A hero with a trust bar: insured, satisfaction guarantee, pay after the job, water and power on board
- Four service cards: exterior, interior, paint correction, ceramic coating
- Pricing with a **Car / SUV / Truck or van** switch, plus a ceramic coating offer and add-ons
- How it works, why choose us, service area and an FAQ
- A booking form that shows a live price estimate; the "Book" buttons on each package fill it in

## Swap before using it for a real business

All of this is sample content:

- Business name, logo letter, phone number (555 numbers are fictional), hours and service area
- Every price, time and add-on
- The claims in the trust bar and the FAQ (insured, satisfaction guarantee, 100-gallon tank, $100 ceramic deposit, one-hour confirmation)
- The photos, which are Unsplash stand-ins; use the business's own photos of its work
- The demo note in the footer

Prices live in two places: the cards in the HTML and the `PRICES` list in the script at the bottom of `index.html`.
