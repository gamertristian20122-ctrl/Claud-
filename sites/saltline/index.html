Questions

Do I need to give you water or power? No. The van carries a 100-gallon tank of deionized water and its own generator. We need a parking spot with about a car’s width of room on each side.

What happens when it’s below freezing? Below 35°F we don’t wash outside. If you have a garage, we work in it with the door cracked. If not, we move your booking to the next day above 35°F, at no charge.

How long does it take? A maintenance wash takes about an hour and a half, the salt reset about three and a half hours, and a full detail most of a day. The quote above shows the time for your car.

Do I need to be home? Only at the start and the end. Leave the keys in a lockbox if you like, and we’ll text you when the car is done.

Will you come to my office? Yes, if your building allows it. Ask your property manager first; we bring everything, so the lot only has to let us park.

What if it rains? Light rain is fine for an interior or a salt flush. For sealant and coatings we need a dry day or a garage, so we reschedule, free.

Why deionized water? Tap water carries minerals that dry into white spots. Deionized water has them taken out, so the car dries without spotting, even in sun.

How do I pay? Card or cash when we finish. Nothing up front, except a $100 deposit to hold a ceramic coating day.

How far do you drive? Anywhere in the Omaha metro, Council Bluffs included. Past that, text us and ask.

Can you get dog hair out? Yes. It’s a $40 add-on, because it takes about half an hour more with rubber brushes and a strong vacuum.
