# Saltline Mobile Detailing (demo site)

A one-page website for a **fictional** Omaha mobile car detailer, built as a portfolio demo.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or drag this folder onto [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) to put it online. There's no build step.

## What's on the page

- **Hero:** "Lose the salt line." The black and grey fields meet at a seam that cuts through the headline, and the seam slides down as you scroll, as if the salt were washing off.
- **The Omaha year:** salt, pollen, hail, cottonwood, bugs and sap. The current month's season turns orange automatically.
- **A visit, as a receipt:** what happens, in order, on a Saturday salt reset.
- **Price your detail:** a working quote builder (vehicle, package and add-ons give a price and a time). It opens on this month's recommended package.
- **Questions, booking and footer.**

## Swap before using it for a real business

All of this is sample content:

- Business name, phone number (555 numbers are fictional), hours and service area
- Every price and time in the packages, add-ons and receipt
- The 100-gallon tank, the 35°F garage rule, the $100 ceramic deposit and the same-day reply promise
- The Unsplash detailing photos, which stand in for the business's own photos of its work

The seasonal notes (brine in winter, cottonwood in June and so on) are general Omaha facts. Have the owner check them.
